# Artwork licensing and source-quality audit

Verified: 2026-10-29. Reference snapshot: `c9c34bf0b8478bf1c156b41c0568f475f008d272` (`reference/v99256`). Nine originals were read and visually inspected individually from that snapshot; no reference files were altered.

## Scope and decision standard

This is an engineering and preliminary licensing review, not a qualified legal opinion or worldwide clearance. A filename containing “official” does not prove an official source, authorship or permission. Source download histories/permissions for the nine reference PNGs were not supplied. No new third-party logo is approved for public bundling by this audit. Original backgrounds do not license a reproduced mark; vectorization, upscaling, tracing and AI recreation do not remove underlying rights.

- **A — Verified for bundling:** exact file and exact source/WGT redistribution supported by documented rights and conditions.
- **B — Conditional:** possible permission route or legal assessment, but not cleared for bundling.
- **C — Local-only:** outside public releases; user must have a lawful basis for personal use. Not a personal-use exemption.
- **D — Excluded:** insufficient rights or policy excludes the proposed use. A later express authorization may change this decision.

## International analysis (2026-10-29)

Copyright and trademark are separate; one work can be protected by both. Distribution on GitHub and installation inside a WGT both make copies; source-code licenses cannot sublicense a brand owner’s unrelated rights. Free/noncommercial use is not automatically exempt. Territory, purpose, downstream copying, modification, attribution, expiry and revocability must all be evaluated for each authorization.

| Territory | Primary evidence | Engineering consequence |
|---|---|---|
| EU | [Regulation 2017/1001, Article 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/1001/oj/eng); [Directive 2001/29, Articles 2–5](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32001L0029) | Honest referential trademark use may be permitted. This does not supply reproduction/distribution rights in artistic logos or a worldwide exception. |
| US | [Copyright Office visual artists](https://www.copyright.gov/engage/visual-artists/), [originality](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/), [derivative works](https://copyright.gov/circs/circ14.pdf), [fair-use FAQ](https://copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html); [Ninth Circuit model instructions §15.25](https://www.ce9.uscourts.gov/jury-instructions/sites/default/files/WPD/Civil_Instructions_2024_09_0.pdf) | Simple lettering may lack copyright originality; graphic expression can qualify. Statutory descriptive fair use is not the whole nominative-use doctrine. Referential use depends on context and judicial circuit; no automatic right to redistribute logo files or imply endorsement. |

This is a cautious design choice, not a legal guarantee. Local device artwork is displayed on that device and is not copied into public packages; this does not relabel Samsung/app artwork as MIT.

## Alternative source review

- **Official developer libraries:** Alexa, Netflix and Rakuten sources inspected above provide restricted/program/approval-based assets; no worldwide public-source plus WGT grant established. No new A-class brand file.
- **Simple Icons:** [disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md) expressly separates project CC0 from individual icon rights and trademark guidelines. Per-icon source metadata must be checked. The legacy tag/data fetch for 16.35.0 failed, then the official `data/simple-icons.json` at develop commit `90646e4bf7f3b89df6c37e65baf3e7bc759a517c` was retrieved and inspected (SHA-256 `f259024458ed00194c7954bcbf8f0511922adff723e9141df6874d713084e12c`). Netflix and NOW entries contain no explicit per-icon license; Rakuten points to restricted official media guidelines. No exact Alexa, Disney+, Amazon Luna, QVC+, RaiPlay or Xbox entry was found in this current dataset. Lunacy and Rakuten Kobo are unrelated and must not be substituted. No one of these facts clears an icon; none of the nine was adopted based on collection license. Existing source `logos.js` paths remain a pre-existing separate audit scope, not newly certified. The reviewed test WGT explicitly excludes that file and its script tag. Studio’s new generic fallback does not use these paths.
- **Wikimedia individual files:** [Netflix 2015 wordmark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Netflix_2015_logo.svg), [Disney+ logo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney%2B_logo.svg), [Xbox emblem](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Xbox_one_logo.svg) were inspected individually. They carry public-domain threshold assessments plus trademark warnings. Those community assessments are not owner grants, are not all-jurisdiction judgments, and do not clear the exact modified reference PNGs. No bytes imported.
- **Official OSS repositories:** software licensing is not presumed to include owner logos. No exact brand-file license with adequate redistribution evidence was identified for these nine. Empty verified registry is intentional, not evidence that no lawful permission could ever be obtained.
- **Samsung local artwork:** retains exact current candidates and quality. No scraping, export, protected downloads or copies in public releases.
- **Original AeroTizen designs:** the newly written CSS geometry and neutral SVG masters in `docs/artwork/` use no traced logos, imported font files or external images. Released under the repository MIT license, preserving its notice. This permission covers the new composition only, not names/marks or user images.
